The audiobook notice on Books, in two states

A frosted window the first time someone opens Books, then the same words inline on every later visit. Each is drawn at 390 and 1440 wide, over a stand-in of the Books page and the shell.

  • The window is the shipped glass slab (15px blur, tint at 25%, the gradient ring and the 9-layer shadow). Behind it, a veil blurs and dims the whole page, sidebar, header, event log and phone bars included.
  • Okay is its own timer. A solid blue fill grows across the button over the 15 seconds, so the wait shows without reading the number. When the fill is full, the button is the normal blue one.
  • The inline notice keeps today's quiet panel. It is only as tall as the full text needs: two columns from 720px wide, 14px type, tight paragraphs.
  • One blue button per state: Okay. Don't show again is the quiet style. "Your Server" stands in for the site's name.
  • Wide frames are scaled to fit your window; the caption says by how much. Phone frames scroll like a phone. Books, titles and events behind the notice are stand-ins.

Accessibility

What the build has to do. The try-it frames above behave this way.

A dialog, modal

The window is role="dialog" with aria-modal="true", named by its title (aria-labelledby). Everything behind it is inert while it is open, so neither a pointer nor a screen reader reaches the page. Built on the shared WSUI.modal (ui.js), which needs one new option: Escape and the backdrop are ignored until the count ends.

Focus trapped, then returned

Focus starts on the title (tabindex="-1") so the words are read from the top. Tab moves between the words' scroll area (on a phone) and Okay, and never leaves the window. When it closes, focus goes to the page's main heading, since nothing opened it.

The count, said once

One polite live region says "Okay will work in 15 seconds." when the window opens, and nothing after. The number on the button is aria-hidden, so the button's name is always "Okay" and no second is read out. While waiting it is aria-disabled="true" rather than disabled: it stays reachable and is heard as "Okay, dimmed".

Reduced motion

Opacity only. The window fades in without the 6px rise, and the button's fill does not sweep: Okay sits on its dim track and turns solid when the count ends. The number still counts, since that is text, not motion. The blur is not motion and stays.

Contrast and targets

The words are the text colour on the frost over a dimmed page. White on the dim Okay track is about 9:1 and on the solid blue 7:1. Okay is 44px tall (full width on a phone). The inline buttons are 40px on a phone and 36px wide, all over the 24px floor. Focus rings are the site's 2px text-colour ring.

Inline notice

A section named by its h2, as today. When Okay or Don't show again hides it, focus moves to the Books heading so it is not lost. The Tickets page is a real link here; in the window it stays plain text, so the window has one way out.
