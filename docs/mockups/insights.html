Insights, the admin’s view of reading and listening

Every section of the page, the person and book dialog, and every state, each drawn at 390 and 1440 wide inside the shell. The page has no blue button: the period picker, Close and Try again are all the quiet outlined style.

  • Plex app time is hatched wherever it is drawn (bars, the split bar, the legend) and called “an estimate” in words beside it, so it never reads as a measured figure.
  • Each chart names its tallest value on a faint line at its top, so the bars have a scale without a full axis. Hover any bar in the build for its figures in words.
  • On a wide screen, Hours listened leads the Trends section with Pages read and Active people stacked beside it, and People rows lay out in three columns. On a phone everything stacks.
  • The people and books are made up; one name holds <b>markup</b> to show it stays text. Wide frames are scaled to fit your window, and the caption says by how much.

Ninety days chosen. Right now has two people listening (one playing in the web player, one paused in Plexamp) and one reading. People has five rows: one with five books in progress, one with a 300-character title, one whose name holds markup. Every frame is drawn at full length.

Getting there

Admins only. On a wide screen the link sits just above Settings in the sidebar; on a phone it is a row in the More sheet, and the More tab is lit while you are on it. Members never see either.

A person or a book

A row in People opens that person; any book in Trends, Books, Habits or a person’s list opens that book. A full-height sheet on a phone, a centred box from 640px up, both scrolling inside. Close, Escape or a click on the dim area closes it and focus goes back to the row that opened it.

For the build

Where this mockup differs from the code in Tasks 8 and 9 as planned. Ids, data hooks and the rest of the words are unchanged.

  • 1Hatched estimate. The Plex part of every bar, the split bar and the legend swatch use ins-est (a 135° hatch of the text colour, in app.css) instead of bg-frosted-blue/35. Stacked parts have a 2px gap and the top part has 4px rounded corners.
  • 2Peak line. Each bar chart gets a dashed hairline at its top, labelled with the tallest value (“40 hr 30 min”, “1,020 pages”, “5 people”).
  • 3Trends layout. From lg: Hours listened in a 3fr column, Pages read and Active people each week stacked in a 2fr column. Bar heights: Hours listened 160px (216px from lg), the other two 112px (80px from lg).
  • 4People rows. From lg the row is three columns (name and last active, time listened, books) instead of one stack. Each book’s percent is dimmer than its title so the pairs read as pairs.
  • 5Heatmap. A visible line under it, “In your time zone. Plex app listening in it is an estimate.”, and a Less to More key, since the estimate rule covers every figure that includes Plex.
  • 6Abandoned. The date reads “last touched 14 Nov 2026” rather than the bare date, since every abandoned book is at least 30 days old and ago() always returns a date there.
