Request access, inside the sign-in card

Every step a visitor can see, and the new Settings tab, each drawn at 390 and 1440 wide. A picture for review: the buttons don't go anywhere.

  • The glass is the shipped default (15px blur, tint at 25%) and the card is today's sign-in card. A dark gradient stands in for the artwork.
  • Only the part under the logo changes from step to step. The words come from the spec (section 9) and the build plan.
  • Approved by Jordan on 10 October 2026, with every main button in the site's blue.
  • Wide frames are scaled to fit your window; the caption says by how much. People, notes and libraries are made up.

Sign-in card

The card keeps its size, glass and logo. Each step moves focus to its heading. Every step has one blue main button; the other ways out are quiet text buttons. Errors show in one line just above the buttons.

Card left and right

From 1024 wide, Settings > Pages can put the card on the left or the right. Shown with S3, the tallest step. Narrower screens always centre it.

Settings > Access requests

A new tab after Sign-in, for admins. Its badge counts the people waiting and is read as "Access requests 3 waiting". The app's sidebar and phone bars are drawn as plain stubs.
