Tools for finding problems with the site.

Debug mode

Lets admins add ?ws-debug=leaks to a page’s address to check for pages that leave work running after you move on. Members never get it. Leave it off unless you are looking into a problem.

Font

Font
 

Used on every page. Any font from fonts.google.com works.

Frosted glass

Menus, pop-ups, dialogs, the search bar and the sign-in card are frosted glass. Choose how it looks.

Tint
 

How strongly the glass is coloured. The default is .25.

Blur
 

0 px is clear glass. The default is 15 px.

Highlight
 

The light along the top edge and the bright border.

Sheen
 

A soft wash of light from the top-left corner. Off by default.

Grain
 

A fine etched texture on the glass. Off by default.

Shadow depth
 

The shade inside the glass and the shadow under it.

Saturation boost
 

How much richer the colours behind the glass look.
