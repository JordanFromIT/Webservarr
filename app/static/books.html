Before you start listening

For the best audiobook experience, I highly recommend listening right here on this site.

Audiobooks also show up in Plex and Plexamp, and your place should sync between them. Getting that sync right took a lot of work on its own. But those apps are built for music, and some of their problems are outside my control:

  • They don't save your progress reliably
  • Books show up as albums of tracks, with no series or reading order
  • They have fewer features than you get here

This Books page took many hours to build. Behind it is custom logic for the new ebook and audiobook library, and much of that time went into one goal: you should never lose your place in a book. There are several failsafes, a saved listening history, and alerts if anything goes wrong.

I also built a custom ebook reader, and connected n8n, Kavita and Plex behind the scenes so book requests just work.

So I can keep improving it, I can see what's read and listened to here.

If something isn't right, please open a ticket on the Tickets page with as much detail as you can.
